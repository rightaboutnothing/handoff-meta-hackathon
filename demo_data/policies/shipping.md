# Shipping policy

SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA. Acme Filtration Pty Ltd is fictional. Every person, contact, account, product and business fact below is fake. Do not use operationally.

Western Australia metro delivery target: 3 business days. Other Australian destinations: 7 business days.
Free standard freight applies to orders of at least AUD 1500 excluding GST. Below that threshold, standard freight is AUD 35.
Custom product lead time is 15 business days. The dispatch cutoff is 14:00 AWST; later orders enter the next business day's queue.
